Job AI-sistant

Job AI-sistant helps job seekers manage their job search process efficiently. It generates a fluent cover letter based on a template and a job description — essentially a form that builds an effective prompt for ChatGPT. You fill in job details (title, company, location, mission, focus areas), it produces the prompt and resulting cover letter, and you can save results to a local database to track what you've applied to.

Built during the 2022/2023 tech layoffs, when recruiters were overwhelmed with irrelevant applications — the tool nudges applicants to actually read and tailor to the job description rather than mass-applying.
