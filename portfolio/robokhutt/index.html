RoboKhutt (خط)

Robo Khutt is an innovative project that leverages advanced diffusion models to generate high-quality Arabic calligraphy — combining the artistry of traditional calligraphy with the power of AI. The name combines "Robo" for AI/robotics and "Khutt" (خط), the Arabic word for calligraphy.

The approach trains a model to render small sentences or even single words, using the rendered image to feed the diffusion model, starting simple and scaling up to more complex text generation. It's an ongoing, incremental effort merging the elegance of Arabic calligraphy with modern generative AI.
